How your messages are protected
  • Every message and photo is encrypted in your browser (AES-256-GCM) with your vault key before it is uploaded.
  • Anyone who knows the vault key can read the chat. Your database host cannot.
  • PINs are a lock for this app, not server authentication. Share the vault key only with people you trust.
